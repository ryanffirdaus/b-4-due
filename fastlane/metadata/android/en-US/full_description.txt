B4Due reminds you of bills and subscriptions before they are due: electricity, water, internet, BPJS health insurance, loans, vehicle tax, insurance, streaming services and more. Built for Indonesia, with an Indonesian-first interface and English included.

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No account, server, ads or analytics.</li>
<li>Your data stays on your phone. The internet is only used to download exchange rates, with no personal data.</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Reminders 7, 3 and 1 day before and on the due date, at the time you choose.</li>
<li>"Paid" and "Remind me tomorrow" actions on the notification.</li>
<li>One-off, monthly, yearly, 5-yearly or every-N-months bills.</li>
<li>Templates for common Indonesian bills.</li>
<li>Prices in foreign currencies (USD, EUR, SGD, JPY…) with a rupiah estimate at today's rate.</li>
<li>Home screen with the monthly total, swipe to mark paid, a bill calendar and spending statistics.</li>
<li>Light and dark mode.</li>
<li>JSON backup, CSV export and Android Auto Backup.</li>
<li>Optional PIN or biometric app lock.</li>
<li>Optional calendar events as a backup reminder.</li>
<li>Battery-saver guide per phone brand.</li>
</ul>
